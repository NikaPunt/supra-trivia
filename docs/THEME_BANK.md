# Theme Bank & Spare Question Vault

Use this file to park overarching monthly themes, clever round mechanics, and standalone lateral clues that don't fit the current month's theme.

## Candidate Monthly Themes

1. **Second Place & Silver Medals ("Almost Famous")**
   * *Concept:* Everything about runner-ups, vice presidents, second-tallest mountains, sequels that outshone the original, and silver.
2. **False Friends & Misnomers**
   * *Concept:* Things named after the wrong place, animals that aren't what they sound like (koala bears, flying foxes, horned toads), historical myths.
3. **The Elements (Earth, Wind, Fire, Water + Periodic Table)**
   * *Concept:* Each round tackles a classical or chemical element across pop culture, geography, food, and science.
4. **Colors & Spectrums**
   * *Concept:* Round 1: Red/Orange, Round 2: Green/Blue, Round 3: Black/White/Gold, Round 4: Hidden Chroma Connections.
5. **Heists, Hoaxes & Con Artists**
   * *Concept:* Famous thefts, counterfeit history, Trojan horses, and pop culture capers.

---

## Round Mechanics Menu (For Round 3/4 Variety)

* **Before & After (Wordplay):** Combine two famous things via a shared overlapping word (e.g., * Wheel of Fortune + Fortune Cookie = Wheel of Fortune Cookie*).
* **Progressive Hidden Connection:** Answers 1–4 share a secret link; Q5 asks *"What connects the first four answers?"*
* **Triangulation / Closest Without Going Over:** Used as Question 10 (Separator) where teams deduce a number from two historical/geographic anchors.
* **Two Truths & A Lie (or Odd One Out):** Give 4 constrained options and ask which one breaks the rule.
